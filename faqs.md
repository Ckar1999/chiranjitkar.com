## 3. Model Release & Paid Photography Service Agreements

### Q22: What is a Model Release Agreement?
**A:** A legally binding contract between Chiranjit Kar Photography (the Photographer) and the Model granting explicit permission to use the photographs for portfolio, digital, print, and commercial display.

### Q23: What is the purpose of a Model Release Agreement?
**A:** It protects both parties by clearly defining photograph usage rights, distribution permissions, and releasing the photographer from claims arising from lawful image display.

### Q24: What rights does the Model grant to the Photographer?
**A:** An irrevocable, non-exclusive, worldwide right to display, publish, reproduce, and exhibit the photos in online portfolios, social media, marketing collateral, and professional exhibitions.

### Q25: Is the Model Release Agreement applicable to both adult models and minors?
**A:** Yes. For individuals under 18 years of age, a parent or legal guardian must execute the agreement and give consent on their behalf.

### Q26: Can the Model request monetary compensation for the use of their likeness in collaboration shoots?
**A:** For creative collaboration/TFP projects, no monetary compensation is provided unless explicitly agreed upon in writing prior to the shoot.

### Q27: How is the privacy and confidentiality of the Model's information protected?
**A:** All personal identification details, contact records, and unreleased shoot data are treated with strict confidentiality under our privacy policy.

### Q28: What is the governing law for the Model Release Agreement?
**A:** The agreement is governed by the laws of India, subject to the jurisdiction of competent Indian courts.

### Q29: What is the purpose of the Paid Photography Service Agreement?
**A:** It governs client bookings, stipulating service deliverables, payment terms, delivery schedules, cancellation terms, and usage licenses.

### Q30: When does the Client receive the final edited photographs?
**A:** After the shoot is completed, raw selections are submitted, and payment is cleared, final retouched photos are delivered within 1 to 2 months.

### Q31: How long does Chiranjit Kar Photography typically take to deliver final photographs?
**A:** Standard turnaround is 1–2 months, ensuring each image receives dedicated retouching, frequency separation, and color grading.

## 4. Agreement Verification Process

### Q32: What is the Agreement Verification process?
**A:** A secure step to authenticate the identity of the signer and ensure the mutual legality of photography contracts.

### Q33: Why is Agreement Verification necessary?
**A:** It prevents fraudulent submissions, confirms age/identity, and validates the contractual obligations for both parties.

### Q34: How can I initiate the Agreement Verification process?
**A:** Submit your legal name, date of birth, contact details, and complete the verification step via our online portal.

### Q35: What are the available verification options?
**A:** Clients can choose between:
1. **Document-based Verification:** Submitting the last 6 digits of their Aadhaar card and PAN card.
2. **Payment-based Verification:** Making a nominal ₹1 authentication payment through Razorpay.

### Q36: How is document-based verification conducted?
**A:** The partial identification digits verify your legal identity without requiring full document upload, safeguarding your privacy.

### Q37: How is payment-based verification conducted?
**A:** An encrypted ₹1 verification charge is processed via Razorpay, linking verified banking/UPI KYC details to the digital contract.

### Q38: Is the information provided during verification confidential?
**A:** Yes. All data is protected with secure encryption and used solely for contract validation.

### Q39: Is the verification fee refundable?
**A:** The nominal ₹1 verification fee is non-refundable as it covers transaction gateway processing.

### Q40: Can clients proceed with agreements without verification?
**A:** No. Verification is mandatory to complete and validate any legal agreement with Chiranjit Kar Photography.
