FASHION COLLABORATIONS

Bring us an extraordinary idea.

As a fashion photographer, I welcome considered proposals from models and talent, agencies and managers, fashion brands and designers, and other fashion creatives. Share the idea, your work, and how you’d like to collaborate.

For models and talent: unpaid portfolio collaborations
Selected model-led shoots are unpaid: we do not charge the model, and no modeling fee is paid. We accept only concepts with an exceptional, coherent high-fashion moodboard and a distinctive editorial story that can add real value to our portfolio.
For agencies, brands, and creative partners
We review fashion-focused proposals for creative fit, portfolio value, and feasibility. Scope, compensation, expenses, image use, and deliverables are discussed separately and agreed in writing before any project is confirmed.

Open dates are selectable. A proposal does not reserve a date.

Submitting a proposal does not guarantee acceptance or confirm a shoot. We review fashion relevance, creative merit, portfolio value, and feasibility. If selected, we’ll agree the shoot plan and applicable terms in writing before scheduling.
