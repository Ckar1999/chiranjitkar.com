🙏 A Sincere Bow & Appeal to Respected Teachers & Headmasters

Teachers are the true architects of our nation. We salute your selfless dedication, love, and tireless commitment in shaping the character and future of our children.

Because this Foundation is 100% privately self-funded from personal savings and creative work with zero public donations, every rupee is hard-earned and precious. We humbly urge all educators: Please certify and recommend ONLY those students who come from genuinely economically disadvantaged homes and cannot afford syllabus books, notebooks, or basic school clothing.

By reserving our assistance strictly for genuinely needy children, together we ensure that no deserving student drops out of school due to financial helplessness.

To protect philanthropic resources from misuse and ensure study supplies reach genuinely needy children, the Foundation enforces a strict protocol:

📜 Mandatory Requisition Document:

An official letter on school letterhead (or clean white paper) bearing the Official School Rubber Stamp and the signature of the Headmaster or Subject Teacher In-Charge, certifying the student’s enrollment and genuine need.

🔒 1 Application Per Campaign Drive:

Once registered, each student is eligible to apply only ONCE per campaign drive. Duplicate submissions under the same student identity are automatically blocked.

📞 Teacher & Headmaster Intimation:

Upon approval, our desk posts an official intimation letter to the Headmaster and delivers the study package directly to the school for in-person student handover.
