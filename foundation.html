ANTI-SCAM VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

Why We Require Headmaster Certification

To prevent commercial abuse and ensure that study materials reach genuinely deserving students, the Chiranjit Kar Foundation enforces an uncompromising verification standard:

📜 Required Requisition Document:

An official letter on school letterhead (or clean paper) signed by the Headmaster or Subject Teacher In-Charge with the Official School Rubber Stamp stating that the student is enrolled and in genuine need of study materials.

🔒 1 Application Per Student Monthly:

The student’s 12-digit Aadhaar number is verified at submission. No duplicate requests are permitted for the same student within a single calendar month.

📞 Verification Call:

Our desk may place a short WhatsApp or voice call to the recommending teacher's mobile number prior to parcel dispatch.
