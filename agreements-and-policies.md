# Agreements, Terms & Policies

This document details the legal agreements, contractual highlights, and operating policies of Chiranjit Kar Photography.

---

## 1. Photography Agreement Highlights

### A. Collaboration Agreement
* **Purpose:** Collaborative shoots for mutual creative and professional portfolio enhancement.
* **Roles:** Clearly delineated responsibilities for photographer, model, MUA, and styling crew.
* **Usage Rights:** The photographer owns copyright and maintains unrestricted promotional/display rights across digital and print media.
* **Compensation:** Agreed upon per project (TFP / mutual portfolio building, or subsidized collaboration fee).
* **Confidentiality:** Mutual protection of creative concepts, unreleased footage, and contact details.
* **Termination:** Either party may terminate with written notice prior to production initiation.

### B. Model Release Agreement
* **Likeness Permission:** Model grants the photographer perpetual, worldwide rights to capture, edit, display, and publish their likeness.
* **Minors:** Explicit parental/legal guardian consent required for subjects under 18.
* **Compensation Waiver:** Agreement that no royalties or additional monetary claims apply beyond agreed project deliverables.
* **Release of Liability:** Releases photographer from claims relating to lighting, posing, or artistic depiction.

### C. Client Photography Service Agreement
* **Advance & Booking:** Shoots are scheduled upon payment of advance deposit.
* **Delivery Schedule:** Final retouched images delivered within **1 to 2 months** following client image selection and full payment.
* **Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy:**
  * Cancellations require reasonable prior notice.
  * Cancellations made within **48 hours of the shoot** are non-refundable.
  * The advance payment is non-refundable; however, the client may **reschedule the shoot for an alternate available date using the same advance deposit**.
* **Liability Cap:** In the unforeseen event of equipment failure or photographer emergency, liability is limited strictly to a full refund or complimentary reshoot.
* **Governing Law:** All contracts are governed under the laws of India.

---

## 2. Agreement Verification Policy
To execute legally valid agreements online, signers must complete verification through one of two channels:
1. **Document Verification:** Verification using the last 6 digits of Government ID (Aadhaar / PAN Card).
2. **Payment KYC Verification:** A nominal ₹1 transaction processed securely via Razorpay to confirm banking KYC match.
