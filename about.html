Founder & Creative Director

Chiranjit Kar

Delhi • Mumbai • Agartala

6+ Years Specialized Industry Practice

Engineering Precision Meets Haute Couture Aesthetics

Chiranjit Kar is an Indian fashion, commercial, and portrait photographer whose visual style synthesizes mathematical geometry with high-fashion drama. Having graduated with formal engineering credentials in Delhi, Chiranjit brings a rare technical rigor to lighting setups, optical calculations, and color calibration.

Rather than viewing fashion photography simply as documentation of attire, Chiranjit approaches every shoot as an editorial canvas—weaving narrative depth into fabric textures, sculpting facial bone structure with directional lighting, and eliciting authentic, relaxed confidence from every model.

Mentorship Pedigree

Praveen Bhat Academy Alumnus (Delhi)

Chiranjit completed rigorous professional fashion photography training at the prestigious Praveen Bhat Academy in Delhi under celebrity fashion photographer Praveen Bhat and creative mentor Krishan Thapa. Under their direct guidance, he mastered high-end commercial studio lighting, commercial lookbook directing, and master retouching workflows.
